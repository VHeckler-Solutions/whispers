Ethics & Disclaimer

Living Upstream is an educational platform exploring consciousness, metacognition, and emotional clarity. It is not a substitute for medical, psychiatric, or therapeutic treatment.

All exercises, practices, and ideas are offered for educational purposes. Results may vary, and participation is at your own discretion and responsibility.

Awareness is a tool. Responsibility is the frame. Alignment is the path.

Important Guidelines

  • If you are experiencing severe psychological distress, suicidal thoughts, psychosis, or dissociation, seek professional help immediately.
  • All exercises are optional — do not push yourself beyond comfort or safety.
  • Respect your own boundaries and the boundaries of others.
  • Living Upstream promotes awareness, not control over others.
  • Progress requires patience, observation, and self-responsibility.

By continuing, you acknowledge that you engage with this material voluntarily and responsibly.
